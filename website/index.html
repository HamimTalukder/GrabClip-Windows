How it works

Three steps. One less thing to worry about.

No command-line rituals. No juggling separate tools. Take a link, choose what matters, and let GrabClip handle the rest.

Bring a link

Paste a video or playlist URL. Preview the title, source, duration and estimated file size before downloading.

Make it yours

Pick video or audio, choose quality and format, add subtitles or select a specific time range.

Keep it organized

Find finished downloads in the Library, resume playback, organize course lessons and search subtitles where available.

Know what you're downloading before it starts.

GrabClip previews the content first, then lets you choose what to save. Pick a video quality, extract audio, include subtitles, or grab only the segment you actually need.

Your downloads don't need your constant attention.

Queue multiple links, pause or resume interrupted downloads, and let the next item start automatically. Schedule downloads for later, limit your speed while you're studying, or set what happens when the queue finishes.

  1. SchedulePick a start time, for example 02:00.
  2. DownloadThe queue runs by itself. Cap the speed to keep the rest of your internet usable.
  3. FinishDo nothing, sleep or shut down. Keep GrabClip open while the schedule waits.

Downloaded on your PC. Ready for your phone.

Scan a QR code to open a saved video on your phone over the same Wi-Fi network. Need a smaller file? Make a 480p copy or extract an MP3 from a completed video.

Your phone and PC must be on the same Wi-Fi. The link works for 10 minutes, and Windows may ask you to allow GrabClip on private networks.

When a site changes, start with an update, not a reinstall.

GrabClip includes an option to update yt-dlp, the download tool it relies on for many supported sites. The app also checks for new GrabClip releases and offers a built-in update. Every update is signed, so only genuine GrabClip files can be installed.

Your PC. Your files.

Your links stay yours.

GrabClip doesn't require an account and doesn't track activity inside the app. Your URLs and downloaded files aren't uploaded by GrabClip. Imported browser cookies stay on your PC, where you can remove them whenever you need to.

  • No account. Install it and use it.
  • No tracking inside the app. No ads, and no activity sent to the developer.
  • Cookies stay on your PC. Remove them from Settings at any time.

GrabClip communicates with the source websites you choose and with GitHub when checking for updates. Read the privacy page

Questions people ask first

Is GrabClip free?

Yes. GrabClip is free for personal use. Read the licence for the applicable terms.

Does GrabClip upload my links or downloaded files?

No. There are no accounts and no tracking inside the app. GrabClip only talks to the websites you download from, and to GitHub when it looks for an update. Read the privacy page.

Does GrabClip work on macOS or Linux?

Not at the moment. GrabClip supports Windows 10 and Windows 11 on 64-bit systems.

Why does Windows show a security warning?

The current installer is not signed with a paid Windows code-signing certificate, so SmartScreen may show a warning. Download only from the official GitHub release and compare the SHA-256 hash first. Read more.
