Frequently askedquestions.

Quick answers about compatibility, downloads, privacy and common problems. For step-by-step guides, visit the Help Center.

General

Is GrabClip free?

Yes. GrabClip is free for personal use. Read the licence for the applicable terms.

Does GrabClip work on macOS or Linux?

Not at the moment. GrabClip supports Windows 10 and Windows 11 on 64-bit systems.

Do I need Python or a command-line tool?

No. GrabClip includes its download engine and installs without a separate Python or command-line setup.

Which websites can I download from?

YouTube, Facebook, Instagram, TikTok and other sites supported by its download engine. Availability can change when websites update their systems, and some content requires you to sign in.

Is the source code public?

No. The source code is not public. GrabClip is free for personal use, and the bundled open-source tools keep their own licences.

How can I support GrabClip?

Open Settings, then Help, then Support the developer, or visit the support section. A star on GitHub or sharing the app with a friend also helps a lot.

Downloads

Can I download an entire playlist or course?

Yes. GrabClip can organize a playlist into a course folder with numbered lessons. You can track watched lessons and check for new lessons later. How to download a course.

Can I download only part of a video?

Yes. In the last step of Add download, turn on Download only a part and enter a start and end time, for example 12:30 to 15:10. It is not available for course downloads.

Can I download audio without the video?

Yes. Choose Audio only, then pick Best, MP3, AAC, OPUS or FLAC.

Can I download multiple links at once?

Yes. Paste up to 50 links, one per line, or drag a link onto the app window.

Can I download private videos?

GrabClip can use cookies exported from your own browser to reach content that your logged-in account is allowed to see. Access still depends on your permissions and the website's restrictions. Never share your cookies file. How to import cookies.

Can I download DRM-protected videos or torrents?

No. GrabClip does not support DRM-protected videos or torrents.

Privacy and security

Does GrabClip upload my links or downloaded files?

No. There are no accounts and no tracking inside the app. GrabClip only talks to the websites you download from, and to GitHub when it looks for an update. Read the privacy page.

Where does GrabClip store my settings and history?

Settings, history, the saved queue and cookies are stored in %APPDATA%\GrabClip. Downloaded files go to the output folder you choose in Settings.

Why does Windows show a security warning?

The current installer is not signed with a paid Windows code-signing certificate, so SmartScreen may show a warning. Download only from the official GitHub release and compare the SHA-256 hash first. Read more.

Why does my antivirus flag the installer?

Some security programs flag installers that bundle tools such as yt-dlp and FFmpeg. Check the SHA-256 hash against the official release, and look into any detection you cannot explain before you run the file.

Troubleshooting

A website used to work but no longer downloads. What should I try?

Open Settings, then Updates, then Update yt-dlp. This fixes many website problems without a new version of GrabClip. If it still fails, report it with the error details, and never attach your cookies file.

Why is my download slow?

Turn on Fast download in the last step of Add download. Speed also depends on your network and on the source website. Check that Study mode and the Night scheduler speed limit are off.

Why does Send to phone fail?

Your PC and phone must be on the same Wi-Fi network. Allow GrabClip in the Windows Firewall prompt if Windows asks, and start sharing again if ten minutes have passed.

How do I uninstall GrabClip?

Open Windows Settings, then Apps, and uninstall GrabClip. Your downloaded videos are never touched.
